output (VPC, key pairs) are the expected PASSING results -- they confirm the
resources are gone.

Terminated instances remain queryable via describe-instances and the Resource
Groups Tagging API for roughly an hour after destruction. A clean teardown shows
every instance in state "terminated", not an empty result.
